## Opgave
Implement particles, cell shatter, screen shatter and motes (CONTRACTS.md §7, PLAN.md §4 cinematic + §6 shatter). Particles: pooled Pixi v8 ParticleContainer + Particle, procedural textures generated once (soft dot, spark streak oriented along velocity, snow flake, ember, shardlet, glint star), additive blending, per-kind physics, setBudget for quality tiers, allocation-free update. CellShatter.burst(tex, cx, cy, size, {warm}): warm=true → split the symbol texture into ~9 Voronoi pieces (3 precomputed patterns, pick by crand) rendered as small textured Meshes (Pixi batches small meshes) that burst outward with spin, gravity, fade, additive rim light and a few sparks; warm=false (RETURN profile) → a desaturated, dim dissolve in place with NO shards, NO sparks. Must handle 64 simultaneous bursts smoothly. ScreenShatter.start(frameTexture, impact): 48 Voronoi cells (denser near impact), crackReveal 0..1 draws glowing cracks (#FF2BD6→#FFFFFF) propagating from the impact along cell edges (dash reveal by distance), then shards separate and fly outward toward the camera (scale 1→1.8 fake z, ±4 rad/s spin, gravity, additive rim + slight chromatic edge), with 300 debris particles; shard UVs map the captured frame so the real image breaks apart. Store shards in normalized coords so resize is safe. Motes.launch(from,to,n,{color,dur,onArrive}): glowing #CFEFFF motes travelling a curved field line with a helical offset and short trails (650 ms default), pooled. Harness dev/particles.html: deterministic stepping via hash #t= (advance fixed dt from 0, render once) showing each system mid-flight, including ScreenShatter of a captured test scene at t=0.2/0.6/1.0 s after start.
