## Opgave
Implement the world/sky (CONTRACTS.md §3, PLAN.md §6 render graph). This is the hero backdrop and the progression bar: the aurora must visibly grow Kp 0→9 via tiers.skyAt(kp) (intensity, speed, fold, red, violet, stars, crackle). Technique: bake static layers once per resize into an RT (sky gradient #050B1A→#0B1B3A with faint airglow #0F3B2E near horizon, Milky Way fbm band, hash stars with twinkle factor stored for a cheap live twinkle, Møns Klint: 2–3 ridged cliff silhouette layers with chalk faces (#C9D3E6 at low alpha) that are lit by the aurora colour at runtime via a stored face mask, tree line on top); render the AURORA CURTAINS live at 0.5× resolution into an RT: 3 curtains, each a domain-warped 1D curve across the sky with a SHARP bright lower seam and exponential fade upward, vertical ray striations scrolling along the curtain, folds, colour by height (557.7 nm green at the seam → red 630 nm at the top when 'red' > 0, violet fringe with 'violet'); then composite at full res: upscaled aurora (additive over sky), sea below horizonY mirroring the sky+aurora (flipped, ripple displacement, −55% brightness, streak highlights), horizon haze, blue-noise/hash dithering to kill banding. glow param lifts aurora ≤10% luminance. STORM (storm→1): sky blends to crimson void #0A0204→#3A0010, curtains become crimson #FF1E3C/magenta #FF2BD6 and violent, a plasma sun rises with 'sun' (limb darkening, granulation, polar-fbm prominences, cheap in-shader rays). CME (cme 0..1): a white-hot→crimson plasma front (polar fbm edge) sweeps from the top down the screen. envColors() returns the 3 dominant aurora colours right now. Must hold 60 fps on mid-range phones: aurora ≤ ~2 ms at half res, composite cheap. Harness dev/sky.html with hash params #kp=&storm=&cme=&sun=&t= ; screenshot Kp 0,3,5,7,8,9, storm=1 sun=0.6, cme=0.5 at 390x844 and 1280x720. Art-direct against real photos of the May 2024 aurora over Denmark: green curtains with pink/red tops, sharp lower edge, rays.
