NORMATIVE RULES (document at top of engine.ts; test each):
- Base grid 6x6, storm 8x8. Column-major idx = col*rows+row, row 0 top. Symbols per types.ts SYM. Cells drawn independently from weights (no SUN in weights). Suns: initial drop only, per column independently with prob pSun (pSunStorm in storm), at a uniformly random row of that column (replacing the drawn symbol). Suns never removed; they fall with gravity like other survivors; refills never contain suns.
- Clusters: for each paying symbol s (0..6), orthogonal flood fill over cells that are s or WILD; a cluster must contain ≥1 cell of s; size ≥5 pays paytable[s][bucket(size)] where buckets 5,6,7,8,9-10,11-12,13-15,16+. Wilds may count in several clusters. Pure-wild groups do not pay.
- Marks ("reading A"): each cluster win uses marks BEFORE this step's upgrade: mult = max(1, Σ marks≥2 over the cluster's cells) (additive). After evaluating a step, every cell in the union of winning clusters upgrades one level: 0→1(frost)→2→4→8→16→32 (base cap baseMarkCap=32; storm cap 128); cells holding a WILD that was part of a win upgrade TWO levels. Marks belong to positions (not symbols); base marks reset each spin; storm marks persist the whole storm.
- Cascade: remove union of winning cells, survivors fall (report moves from→to), refill from top with fresh weighted draws (report refill cell+sym). Repeat until no clusters. The final Step has clusters=[] and empty moves/refill.
- Win øre: winOre = round(payX * payScale(or stormPayScale) * mult * stakeOre) — define ONE integer rounding rule (round half up per cluster) and use it identically in sim. Spin total capped at maxWinX*stake (capped=true).
- Charge (base & perk spins only): each removed cell low=1, high=2, wild=3 per removal event; exactly 3 suns → +sunCharge (150) and sunPayX (3×) pay; 4+ suns → triggers.stormB (no sun pay, no sun charge). No charge in storm.
- Anticipation (fixed, logged): if the first k columns (left→right) contain exactly 3 suns in total and there are remaining columns, anticipation = {fromCol: k} for the first such k (the columns after it slow down in presentation).
- Meter: charge + stakeSumOre (Σ charge_i × stake_i). lockedStakeOre = floor(stakeSumOre/charge). addCharge returns tiers crossed (use tiers.ts TIERS fractions × K and kpFromCharge) and stormA when charge ≥ K. Perk "Ladet spin": each time a tier with perk=true (Kp 3,5,7) is crossed, the game grants a FREE extra spin at the locked stake whose marksBefore has 4 random cells at x2 (spinBase with opts.perk). Perk spins still accrue charge (at locked stake).
- Storm routes: A = meter reaches K → storm at locked stake, then meter resets to 0. B = 4+ suns in a base or perk spin → storm at that spin's stake, meter kept. A and B on the same spin → ONE storm at the spin's stake and the meter resets. Storm triggers are evaluated after the spin.
- Storm: stormSpins=10 spins, 8x8, stormWeights fixed, starts with stormStartMarks=6 random cells at x2. Before storm spin index 3, 7, 11, 15 (i.e. every 4th spin): Stormbølge doubles every mark ≥2 (cap 128) — report in meta.wave. 3+ suns in a storm spin → +retriggerSpins (3), max total maxStormSpins=20. Storm suns never pay. Storm win total capped at maxWinX × stake over the whole storm. finishStorm applies the guarantee: if winOre < guaranteeX(30)×stake → guaranteeOre = difference (shown separately).
- RNG: xoshiro128** seeded per spin from splitmix32(sessionSeed ^ DOMAIN_CONST[domain]) advanced by idx (document the exact derivation + call order at the top of engine.ts). spinId format NL-<seed hex8>-<domain letter><idx 6 digits>.
