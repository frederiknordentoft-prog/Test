## Opgave
Implement procedural symbol art (CONTRACTS.md §2, PLAN.md §6 symbol recipes). Everything baked by shader meshes into RenderTextures. Low crystals: faceted gem shader — L1 trillion #5CE1FF, L2 princess (square rotated 45°) #3DFFB0, L3 hexagonal brilliant #8A5CFF, L4 pear/teardrop #FF5C9A — each: outline polygon, table polygon ≈0.52 scale, crown facets (split sectors into star/kite facets) with per-facet tilted normals (+hash jitter), Blinn spec (exp≈80) from a top-left key light, env reflection from the 3 'env' aurora colours by normal direction, bright facet-edge highlights (distance to facet borders), subtle 3-tap RGB dispersion, inner sparkle, crisp anti-aliased silhouette, soft drop glow. H1 Månen: crescent SDF (circle minus circle), Worley craters, earthshine on the dark part, 22° halo ring with prismatic edge. H2 Polarstjernen: star r(θ)=mix(0.22R,R,|cos 2θ|^6)-style 4 main points + finer diffraction spikes, white-gold #FFF7E0 core, #A8C8FF halo. H3 Rav: noise-displaced pebble SDF, gradient #FFE19A→#FFB547→#B8560A, subsurface glow, 5–8 bubbles, a small fern fossil, crescent specular. WILD Nordlysbue: hexagonal ice plate with a mini aurora inside and 'WILD' lettering (you may bake Pixi Text with gradient fill + glow into the RT). SUN (scatter): limb darkening, granulation, corona filaments (polar fbm), flares, warm gold. STORM edition: obsidian gems (#120408) with ridged-fbm magma veins in each gem's hue family shifted hot, moon → total eclipse with corona, star → white-hot, amber → glowing ember, wild → plasma orb with lightning filaments, sun → crimson. All 9 must read clearly at 56 px cells on a phone (distinct silhouettes = colour-blind safe) and look stunning at 256 px. Also bakeCellFx textures.
