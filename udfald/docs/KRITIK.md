# Red team-kritik af KONCEPT.md

> Arbejdstitlen ØRE er siden ændret til **UDFALD**, og prisenheden til **kurs** (2. nov. 2026). Teksten nedenfor er bevaret, som den blev besluttet.

*Uafhængig kritik af syntese-spec'en. Rettelserne er indarbejdet i PLAN.md, som har forrang.*

## Dom

Partly. The spec is novel in places and can be built, but it is not AAA-safe as written. The math holds up under spot checks: power-method ask 49 from 48/27/25, 55+27+21=103, 510/384/131 shares and 399,52 kr, the LMSR whale moving about −3 øre, trigger 38 % → ask 42 at σ=1,0, and VAR 73/35. Several moments exist in neither Oddset nor Polymarket: the trading halt with a labelled gap and limit orders (Fælder) filled inside it, the VAR split into two possible prices (Schrödinger), the three-branch future zone, Målvejret goal-window contracts, the cash-out fader ("529 kører gratis"), and the handball oscilloscope trigger. The single biggest risk is the composition of the main screen, not the engine. Twelve stacked bands push the new ideas into an 88 px future-zone stub and a 76 px weather strip. The top half is a Polymarket chart (øre as %, 5m/15m pills, activity tape, volume). The bottom 40 % is an Oddset coupon (three 1X2 tiles with odds, market tabs, stake chips, "Kurv (0)"). The first 5 seconds will read as "Polymarket on top of Oddset". The second risk is the demo: the JSON timeline, the narrative table and AC21 disagree, and four scripted gestures land inside fast-forward segments or trading halts, so demo90 cannot run as written.

## Risiko: ligner en standard-sportsbook

HIGH for y 475–810, MEDIUM-HIGH for being read as a Polymarket clone. Standard-sportsbook signals: (1) three equal FCK/X/BIF tiles with "1,82 · sælg 53" — the most recognisable Oddset/Bet365 element; (2) "Udfald · Næste mål · Mål" market tabs; (3) the bet slip has stake chips 25/50/100/250/500, a payout line and a 64 px Køb button; (4) "Kurv (0)" in the bottom nav is a bet-slip counter; (5) scoreboard header. Polymarket signals: price in øre = % chance ("54 af 100" ≈ "54 % chance"), a line chart with time-range pills, an activity tape, an "Oms." volume line, LMSR, buy/sell shares with average price. The pitch's differentiator "sell any second" is weak because Oddset already has Cash Out. What actually differs: halts and gaps, the branch fan, Målvejret, the fader, the pace line (Spøgelseslinjen), move grades. Today these take less than 25 % of the screen, and two of them only show after a tap or at the end. Fix: make the future zone and halts the dominant visual. Replace the three tiles with one segmented 100-øre bar. Remove the tabs and the "Kurv" counter.

## Risiko: overload

HIGH. The main screen stacks 12 bands in 844 px: Kampbånd, Kampstriben, Udsigten, head, canvas, Båndet, tabs, tiles, Målvejret, position card, bottom bar, plus a 24 px cause chip. That is about 30 live numbers, with the same ask/odds/bid shown twice and "55" shown four times. At least six independent motion sources run outside the hero canvas: the ball dot changing zone about 4×/s, the tape at 2 Hz, digit rolls on about 10 numbers at up to 4 Hz with 350 ms rolls (so they never settle), the Målvejret sawtooth, damage drain, and the chroma shift. The hero canvas is only 232 px (27 %). On a goal, about 15 things change within 1,5 s, including a second ring. In the Claude iOS app the iframe is shorter than 844 px and has no safe-area insets, so the bottom bands, which carry the payout, overflow. Target budget: ≤ 7 bands, ≤ 12 live numbers, ≤ 3 animating regions outside the canvas, hero canvas ≥ 300 px.

- **MAJOR · Motion without visible cause (noise, drain):** Logit noise ε has stationary sd 0,016 ≈ 0,4 pp (×1,5 in danger phases ≈ 0,6 pp). - **MAJOR · Cause attribution (§8.7):** Thresholds disagree: 1 øre in principle 2 and in the chip rule, 2 øre in AC11. The window is 10 s of real time, but the copy cites game time ('3 hjørnespark på 4 min'); under ×16, 10 s equals 40 game minutes. '▲ 6 øre · 5 min' has no stated unit.   - *Krævet ændring:* Attribute in game time over a 2-game-minute window. - **MAJOR · One simulation? Feed vs price model vs MC:** The feed makes goals from a zone Markov chain, then shots (base shot rate unspecified), then Beta xG. Prices use a separate λ formula whose c_i is calibrated independently. The closed form holds the score-state multiplier m_score constant over the horizon and ignores the Jensen term of the pressure process (E[e^π] ≈ +12 %). MC updates m_score after simulated goals. Systematic gaps of 1–3 pp, plus MC sampling error (0,7 pp at 5.000 paths, 1,1 pp at 2.000), make AC3 '≤2,5 pp at every result' fail. AC22 calibrates the feed, not the prices.
  - *Krævet ændring:* Use one goal process. The feed draws goals by thinning λ_i(t); shots are cosmetic and conditioned on λ, and every goal is rendered as a shot. Add the lognormal variance correction to the closed form. Compute 1X2 by a 1-minute score-state DP with dynamic m_score (121 states, about 0,1 ms) instead of plain Skellam. MC uses the same λ. Restate AC3 as mean |Δ| ≤ 1 pp and p99 ≤ 3 pp over a match.

Halt and VAR durations run in game time, and any T3 forces ×1. Hit-stop, rings and lerps run on a separate presentation clock.
